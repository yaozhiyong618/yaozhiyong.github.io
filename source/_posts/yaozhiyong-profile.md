## 👨‍🏫 个人简介 | Biography

**姚志勇** 博士现任 **复旦大学管理学院** 教授、博士生导师。
**Dr. Zhiyong Yao** is a Professor of Economics and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Management, Fudan University.

拥有近二十年严谨的教学与学术研究经验，主要从事微观经济理论、博弈论与契约理论、产业经济学、政治经济学以及中国经济改革等领域的前沿研究。
With nearly two decades of academic research and teaching experience, his research focuses on Microeconomic Theory, Game Theory, Contract Theory, Industrial Organization, Political Economy, and Chinese Economic Reform.
